| Pattern     | Purpose                       | EasyExample               |

**Strategy**  | Change algorithm at runtime   | Google Maps route, Payment method     |
**Observer**  | Notify many objects           | YouTube subscribers, WhatsApp group   |
**Command**   | Wrap a request as an object   | TV Remote, Undo/Redo                  |
**COR**       | Pass request through handlers | Customer Support, ATM cash dispensing |
